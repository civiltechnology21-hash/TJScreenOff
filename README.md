<h1 align="center">TJ Screen Off</h1>

<p align="center"><b>One tap. Screen off.</b></p>

TJ Screen Off is a tiny Android app with a single job: tap its icon and your phone locks and the screen turns off, exactly like pressing the power button.

There is no settings page, no menu and nothing to open. Put the icon wherever you like on your home screen and tap it.

> [!NOTE]
> The app has no internet access and collects nothing. It can lock the screen and nothing else.

---

## How it works

| | What you do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Install the app and place its icon on your home screen. | Nothing yet. |
| **2** | Tap the icon for the first time. | Android asks for one permission. Tap **Activate**. The screen locks. |
| **3** | Tap the icon any time after that. | The screen turns off and the phone locks instantly. |

The permission is asked only once.

---

## Install

1. Download `TJ-Screen-Off.apk` to your phone.
2. Tap the file, then tap **Install**.
3. If Android asks you to allow installs from this source, allow it.
4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap **Install anyway**. This appears because the app is not from the Play Store.
5. Open your app list, touch and hold **TJ Screen Off**, and drag it to a spot on your home screen.

Works on Android 5.0 and newer.

---

## First use

Tap the icon. Android shows a short message asking you to activate the app. This is Android's standard "Device admin" permission, the official way for an app to lock the screen. The app only asks for the lock-screen ability.

Tap **Activate**. The screen locks right away.

> [!TIP]
> If you tap **Cancel**, nothing happens. The next tap on the icon will ask again.

---

## Uninstall

**The easy way**

1. Touch and hold the TJ Screen Off icon.
2. Tap **Remove app**.
3. Confirm with **OK** when Android asks.

That is all. Touch and hold works on the home screen, inside a folder and in the app list.

**If you uninstall from Settings instead**

Android may say the app must be deactivated first. Go to **Settings → Security** (or **Privacy**) **→ Device admin apps → TJ Screen Off → Deactivate**, then uninstall as usual.

> [!NOTE]
> Menu names differ slightly between phone brands.

---

## Questions

**Why does Android say "Device admin"?**
That is the name Android gives to the permission needed to lock the screen. TJ Screen Off uses it only for that.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. It has no internet permission.

**Does it run in the background?**
No. It runs for a split second when you tap the icon, then closes.

**Why can't I swipe it away like other apps?**
While the lock permission is active, Android protects the app from being removed by accident. Use **Remove app** as described above.
